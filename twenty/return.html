Subtraction

May you take away
only the barriers you yourself
have built to buffer what
once seemed fragile
from the glory of this moment,
this unrepeatable, never before seen
moment, which dissolves
into eternity, this particular instance
of forever.

May the careful art of subtraction
leave you unburdened,
so that your way is one of joy,
so that you may arrive in the sacred city
and join the celebration
that started the moment
your trust was sufficient enough
to forget, and become again a fool
who thought they could add anything
to the Full.

May you fare well,
dearest friend, beloved forger,
whose fate is written in clear signs:

you will return, now and now and
now forever.
