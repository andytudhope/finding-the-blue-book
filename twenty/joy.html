Resounding Joy

May the music of life,
sounding in every cell,
set you free.

May it lift you,
literally,
this rising wave.

Quieter than silence,
the whole bell ringing
from big bang to right now,

though totally still
inside, a celebration so vast
it leaves the surface

undisturbed,
the barrier between
two seas set,

yet vibrating one
living note, in every cell.
O joyful sound!
