Real Wealth

May we discover our debt
as a call to come together
and find the same loops,
the same connecting cycles,
that signify life itself.

May such connections
help us clear what is owed
to the future and make good
our obligations to those
who walk behind us.

May it be a quiet,
sweeping jubilee,
an ongoing celebration
of life's structure
and all the many ways
in which we depend on others
to let us know we are one.
