Sincerely Said

The secret of sincerity
comes in with the full moon waves.

To be so whole heartedly who you are
that the audience must suspend disbelief,
all of us obligated by the truth
and so welcomed into paradise.

No other
room for the fiction of us.
You and I rolled up
and given back to love.

There is no god but God,
Who was and is right now forever.
